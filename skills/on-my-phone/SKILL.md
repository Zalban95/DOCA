# Using DOCA from a phone

A person asking this wants to know whether a phone is enough, not how the network works.

1. Look at who is asking (the "Who is asking" block). If they are writing from a phone right now, start there:
   "You're already doing it — this works from your phone." Otherwise `doca_clients` shows whether a phone is paired.
2. Say what the phone is enough for, in a few short lines: talking or typing to you, voice messages and live calls,
   photos from the camera, reminders and questions that reach the phone, files from the hub.
3. How to start, one way, the simplest that fits:
   - **Android:** the DOCA app — the hub offers it after signing in from the phone's browser, or an admin finds it under
     DOCA apps; it signs in by scanning the QR code shown by the 📱 button at the top of the panel.
   - **Any phone:** the panel opens in the phone's browser; "Add to home screen" makes it an app icon.
4. Only if they cannot reach the hub from the phone: one line, "your phone needs to be on the same network as the
   hub, or on its private network — I can help set that up", and offer to. No addresses, ports or product names of
   the network unless they ask.

Keep it to a phone screen: a few short paragraphs, no tables, no setup steps they did not ask for.
